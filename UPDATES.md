### v1.1.0 – Typography & Deepened Analytics Update

*   **Expanded Analytical Dictionary:** Added deep-dive definitions for critical cinematic concepts to maintain evaluation consistency across both languages:
    *   **Subtext:** Psychological, sociological, and philosophical layers beneath surface events.
    *   **Authenticity:** Distance from artificiality, commercial anxiety, and performative clichés.
    *   **Character Design:** Psychological consistency in motivations and traumas.
    *   **Impact:** The emotional weight and psychological resonance left during or after the watch (addressing intentional viewer manipulation).
    *   **Permanence:** The film's long-term staying power in memory and feeling over time.
*   **Typography Overhaul:** 
    *   Integrated **Lora** for elegant, book-like serif headings.
    *   Integrated **Inter** for clean, functional UI body text.
*   **Bilingual Refinements:** Synchronized all new analytical categories, tooltips, and structural definitions seamlessly across both English and Turkish interfaces.
